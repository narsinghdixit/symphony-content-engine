## Product Suite — PureRevenue Platform

### PureFees (Fee Billing & Calculation)
- Automates complex billing workflows across products, households, breakpoints, and discounts
- Eliminates manual errors and single points of failure
- Accurately classifies assets, integrates disparate systems, generates trusted invoices
- Reduces billing disputes and processing costs by 60–80%
- Handles standardized fee logic from proposal through billing

### PureRewards (Advisor Compensation & Incentives)
- Manages advisor compensation workflows with embedded validations, tolerance controls, and audit trails
- Aligns compensation with business strategy to drive the right advisor behaviors
- Provides transparent payout dashboards and real-time (or near-real-time) earnings visibility
- Reduces advisor compensation disputes by up to 85%
- Compensates 17,000+ pay recipients monthly across clients

### PureReports (Revenue Analytics & Dashboards)
- Real-time revenue monitoring and performance dashboards
- Finance-ready reporting and explainable outputs
- Reconciles billing and advisor compensation in one view
- Audit-ready reports with data lineage and approval trails
- Aligns Finance, Ops, and advisor teams around consistent numbers

## Who PureFacts Serves

PureFacts works with two primary segments:
1. **Wealth management firms** — RIAs, IBDs, bank-owned wealth, wirehouses ($1B–$100B+ AUM)
2. **Asset management firms** — global asset managers with cross-border distribution ($10B–$500B+ AUM)
3. **Asset servicing firms** — custodians and administrators supporting the above

## Core Value Proposition (one-line)

PureFacts is the enterprise revenue optimization platform that helps wealth and asset managers find, protect, and grow revenue across fees, compensation, and reporting.
