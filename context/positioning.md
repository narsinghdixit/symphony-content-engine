# PureFacts Positioning Reference

---

## Core Positioning Statement

PureFacts is the enterprise revenue optimization platform for wealth and asset management. We unify fee billing, advisor compensation, and revenue analytics into a single governed system so firms can find revenue they are missing, protect revenue they have earned, and grow revenue they are leaving on the table.

This is not billing software. This is revenue infrastructure.

---

## Value Propositions (each with proof)

### 1. Revenue Lift
Firms using PureFacts see up to 10% top-line revenue improvement by closing pricing gaps, eliminating undocumented discounts, and enforcing fee schedule governance.

### 2. Leakage Prevention
Wealth and asset management firms lose 1–5% of EBITDA to billing errors, pricing gaps, and collection failures (EY data). For a firm with $200M in managed revenue, that is up to $10M walking out the door every year. PureFacts finds it and stops it.

### 3. Spillage Control
Revenue spillage — the loss of potential revenue at the front end during pricing, proposals, and onboarding — is a distinct and often larger problem than back-end leakage. PureFacts embeds pricing guardrails and governance into the workflows advisors use daily, converting discretion into discipline.

### 4. Compliance Risk Reduction
FINRA collected $59.8M in fines in 2024. The SEC reported $8.2B in total financial remedies in FY2024. Fee calculation errors, disclosure mismatches, and compensation misalignment are recurring enforcement themes. PureFacts creates audit-ready evidence by design — approval trails, data lineage, and defensible calculations.

### 5. Advisor Alignment
85% reduction in compensation disputes. Real-time payout dashboards replace opaque, delayed reporting. When advisors trust their compensation, they sell more, stay longer, and recruit peers. In a market where 109,000 advisors plan to retire within 10 years, compensation transparency is a recruiting weapon.

### 6. Operational Efficiency
60–80% reduction in billing processing costs. Firms replace manual, spreadsheet-driven billing with automated, governed workflows — reducing headcount dependency, single points of failure, and cycle times. One wealth manager unlocked $13M in annual value. A £750B+ AUM asset manager cut billing cycle times by 30%+.

## Competitive Differentiation

### vs. Point Solutions (BillFin / Redi2 / Envestnet)
- They solve billing. PureFacts solves revenue.
- PureFacts unifies fees, compensation, and reporting. Point solutions handle one silo — firms still have gaps between billing and comp, comp and reporting.
- PureFacts handles enterprise complexity (multi-entity, cross-border, tiered households). Point solutions are built for SMB simplicity.

### vs. Suite Vendors (SS&C Advent)
- Advent is a portfolio management system with billing bolted on. PureFacts is a revenue system built from the ground up.
- Advent billing lacks depth in compensation management, pricing governance, and revenue analytics.
- PureFacts integrates with any portfolio management platform — firms don't have to rip and replace their core system.

### vs. In-House / Spreadsheets
- The real #1 competitor. 72% of firms use manual workarounds for billing complexity.
- Spreadsheets are error-prone, unauditable, and create single points of failure on the person who built them.
- "Your spreadsheet can't show you the revenue you're not collecting."
- Total cost of ownership: hiring, maintaining, auditing manual processes costs more than PureFacts over any 3-year horizon.

### vs. Doing Nothing (Inertia)
- Every quarter a firm waits, 1–5% of EBITDA leaks. That is not a rounding error.
- Regulatory enforcement is increasing, not decreasing. The cost of a fine is 5–10x the penalty itself when you include remediation, oversight drag, commercial friction, and strategic delay (the "Invisible Invoice Stack").
- Fee compression makes every recovered basis point more valuable than it was five years ago.

## Why Now

Four forces converging simultaneously:

1. **Fee compression is structural**: Average advisory fees falling, fund management fees at historic lows (22 bps average per BCG). Every basis point of leakage or spillage hits harder.

2. **Regulatory intensity is rising**: SEC thematic sweeps (marketing rule, off-channel communications, cash sweeps), FINRA enforcement ($59.8M in fines, 2024), FCA and ESMA active. Enforcement is targeting recurring operating practices, not just isolated misconduct.

3. **The advisor talent war is reshaping economics**: 109,000 advisors retiring in 10 years, recruiting deals at 300–400% of trailing production, compensation transparency now a decisive factor in retention and recruiting.

4. **AI and automation have reached the tipping point**: Firms that modernize revenue infrastructure now can compound operational advantage. Firms that wait will face rising costs on a declining margin base.

## The PureFacts Worldview

Revenue is not a byproduct of operations. It is the most governable value driver in a financial services firm. Most firms treat billing as a back-office function and compensation as an HR problem. The firms that win in the next decade will treat revenue as an enterprise system — with the same rigor they apply to risk management and portfolio construction.

PureFacts exists to make that possible.
